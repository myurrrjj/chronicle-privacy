Data Deletion for Chronicle

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Chronicle has no accounts and no server. Everything the app saves lives only on your own device, which means you are always in full control of deleting it — and there is no copy on our side for us to remove.

In short

  • No account, no server, no cloud copy of anything.
  • You can delete all of the app's data yourself, at any time, using any of the steps below.
  • Deleting Chronicle's data never touches your photos and videos — those stay in your library.
  • You can also email us and we will help.

What Chronicle stores

  • A local database of your favourites, the face grouping data used to sort photos by person, and a search index.
  • Your in-app settings (autoplay videos, default view mode).
  • A thumbnail cache so scrolling is fast.

It does not store your photos or videos, and it does not store anything on a server.

How to delete your data

  1. Clear the app's data. On your device, go to Settings → Apps → Chronicle → Storage & cache → Clear storage. This erases the database, the face data, the search index, your favourites and your settings in one step. Your photos and videos are not affected.
  2. Uninstall the app. Uninstalling Chronicle removes everything it ever stored on your device.
  3. Delete individual photos or videos. Inside Chronicle, delete any item you no longer want. Android will ask you to confirm. This removes the file from your device's library — so only do it if you mean to lose the file itself.
  4. Turn off Android backup for the app if you would rather no copy of its data sat in your Google account backup: Android Settings → System → Backup, or your Google account settings. An existing backup is replaced or removed through the same screens.

What is not stored

We do not operate any server and there is no account, so there is no cloud copy of your data. Chronicle has no internet permission at all, which means it cannot transmit your data anywhere, even if asked to.

Face grouping data

Chronicle can group photos by the people in them. The face data that makes this work is created and kept on your device only. Clearing the app's storage (step 1) deletes it completely. It is never uploaded, and the app does not know or record who anyone is.
